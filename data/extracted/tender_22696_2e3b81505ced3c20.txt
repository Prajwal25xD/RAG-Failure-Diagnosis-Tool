26. Scope of Work 
On behalf of the Department of Science & Technology, GIL invites bids from competent and 
professional e-waste recyclers registered with Gujarat Pollution Control Board (GPCB), who 
meet the minimum evaluation criteria as specified in this bid document. 
a. GIL is looking for an end-to-end solution (collection, transportation, dismantling, recycling 
and / or final disposal) from bidders registered with the as per GPCB guidelines currently in 
force and having established facilities for management of e- waste. 
b. The bidder shall hand over all types of storage drives installed in the unusable IT equipment 
to the concerned office and obtain an acknowledgment/sign-off for the same. The bidder 
shall also provide a Certificate of Data Destruction containing the device serial numbers, 
the method of data destruction used (e.g., degaussing, shredding, or overwriting), and the 
date of destruction. In cases where the storage media cannot be physically separated from 
the equipment (e.g., eMMC, soldered SSDs, or embedded storage devices), the bidder shall 
perform certified on-site data destruction and submit photographic evidence along with 
the corresponding Certificate of Data Destruction. 
c. Bidder should follow environmentally sound practices for management of e-waste as per 
GPCB guidelines and e-waste (Management and Handling) Rules, 2022, as notified by 
MoEF&CC, and as amended from time to time.  
d. The services offered should include collection of e-waste from location mentioned in this 
tender document, transporting the same to collection center, recycling facility or final 
disposal. 
e. The bidder shall ensure that, in cases where storage media is provided, secure collection, 
transportation, data deactivation/degaussing, destruction, and disposal of e-waste are carried out 
in accordance with the prevailing guidelines/rules issued by the competent State/Central 
Government authorities. The bidder shall possess the necessary in-house infrastructure for on-
site/off-site services, maintain complete data security and confidentiality throughout the process, 
and provide necessary certificates/confirmations regarding irretrievability of data and final 
disposal of e-waste within 30 days from the date of collection. Bidder shall visit the facility & 
physically inspect the material. Bidder shall be responsible for the collection of the IT assets from 
the location mentioned in this bid & all other required efforts for its transportation till its final 
disposal & shall accordingly quote the rates.  
The details of IT assets required to be condemned / disposed-off is mentioned.
