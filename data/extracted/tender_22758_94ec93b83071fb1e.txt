Scope of Work and Additional Terms & Conditions
The Bidder under the scope of work in this GeM bid is envisaged to design the solution including but not 
limited to Supply, Installation, and Commissioning of Equipment's/hardware, and the software required 
for the successful implementation of the Video Conferencing Solution with Interactive Flat Panel Displays 
at the identified District Headquarters / Secretariat and Taluka Level Offices under various Departments, 
Government of Gujarat (hereinafter 
and technical specifications and the scope of work mentioned in this GeM Bid. The bidder shall be 
responsible for the implementation of the work as defined in the GeM bid. 
Bidder is also responsible for all the components such as: Type-A Video Conferencing Endpoint (Codec-
based, comprising PTZ Camera, Codec, Microphones and Touch Control Panel), Type-B Video 
Conferencing Endpoint (All-in-One Video Bar), 75-Inch Interactive Flat Panel with OPS, 55-Inch Display 
Unit, HDMI Cables, mounting brackets and any other devices/accessories required for the successful 
running of the envisaged solution at each of the identified locations. 
The Bidder is responsible for any other components inadvertently missed out but required as per the 
proposed solution of the bidder and essential for the overall solution to successfully run during the entire 
project duration; the bidder has to consider the same in his proposed solution. TENDERER will not be 
liable for payment of any additional cost outside of the Financial Bid. 
The locations for installation will be the District Headquarters/Secretariat offices (44 Nos.) and Taluka 
level offices (285 Nos.) listed at Annexure-D, and will be finalized in consultation with the respective 
officers of the concerned Department(s) / GIL. 
The complete scope of work would broadly be: design, engineering, supply, testing, installation, 
commissioning & warranty support of all project deliverables across all locations. 
Quoted items are required to be offered with a 5 (Five) year OEM warranty and on-site support. All 
equipment including material to be installed by the Bidder shall be new and the product should not be 
declared end of support for 5 (Five) years. A certificate to that effect should be furnished from the OEM 
on its letterhead confirming the same. All equipment shall conform to the codes, standards and 
regulations applicable and benefit from the usual manufacturer's guarantees. 
Under the GeM portal, while creating the bid, some items may reflect standard 2-year/3-year warranty 
options. However, the TENDERER requires 5 (Five) years' warranty and on-site support for each item 
(excluding cables & accessories). Bidders shall quote the price accordingly for each item considering 5 
years' warranty support. 
Primary Product & OEM Turnover for evaluation: Video Conferencing Codec / PTZ Camera with Codec 
(Type-A) will be considered as the Primary Product for Experience, Past Performance and OEM Turnover 
evaluation. 
Clarification regarding this Tender: Bidders can seek written clarifications up to 2 days before the Bid 
submission date by email to gm-tech@gujarat.gov.in, dgmTech-gil@gujarat.gov.in & mgr-

Performance Guarantee 
 The performance guarantee will be in the form of bank guarantee for the amount equal of 5% 
of the value of the Order towards faithful performance of the contract obligation, and 
performance of the equipment during Warrantee period. In case of poor and unsatisfactory 
field services, THE OFFICE shall invoke the PBG. 
 The Performance Guarantee shall be valid for the duration of warranty period + extra 2 months 
and shall be denominated in Indian Rupees and shall be in the form of an unconditional Bank 
Guarantee issued by any Nationalized Bank including the public sector bank or Private Sector 
Banks authorized by RBI or Commercial Bank or Regional Rural Banks of Gujarat or Co-
Operative Bank of Gujarat (operating in India having branch at Ahmedabad / Gandhinagar) as 
per the G.R. no. FD/MSM/e-file/4/2024/2859/D.M.O. dated 01.05.2025 issued by Finance 
Department or further instruction issued by Finance department time to time in the format 
provided by THE OFFICE to be submitted within 15 working days of receipt of award. 
 The Successful bidder has to submit Performance Bank Guarantee @ 5% of total order value 
within 15 days from the date of issue of Purchase order. 
 In the event that any amount is recovered, adjusted, or deducted from the Performance Bank 
Guarantee (PBG) towards penalties, damages, or any other recoveries under the Contract, the 
Successful Bidder shall reload the deducted amount and restore the PBG to its original value, 
i.e., 5% of the total Contract Value, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date of such deduction or 
from receipt of notice from the Purchaser. Failure to restore the PBG within the stipulated 
period shall constitute a material breach of the Contract and may result in appropriate action, 
including recovery from pending payments, invocation of the remaining PBG, or termination of 
the Contract, as deemed appropriate by the Purchaser. 
6. Service Terms 
The understanding of the comprehensive maintenance under the warranty period is as follows: 
a. In case of failure, the successful bidder needs to repair or replace the faulty part/component/device 
to restore services at the earliest. 
b. The cost of repairing or replacement of the faulty part/component/device has to be entirely borne by 
the Vendor. 
c. All expenses related to the part/component/device, including hiring of specialized technical expertise 
where required, have to be borne by the Vendor as part of comprehensive maintenance. 
d. The Vendor also needs to make alternate arrangements in case of a major failure that may affect 
services for a longer period. 
e. After repairing or replacement of the part/component/device, the Vendor needs to put the same into 
operation. 
f. All costs related to structured cabling, interconnecting cables, power cables, connectors, mounting 
accessories, consumables, and any other items required for complete installation, testing, 


commissioning, and successful operation of the solution shall be included in the Contract Value. No 
separate payment or additional charges shall be payable for such items.
