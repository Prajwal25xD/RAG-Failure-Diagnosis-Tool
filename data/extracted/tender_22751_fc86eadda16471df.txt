4. Installation & Commissioning 
The successful bidder shall be responsible for: 
• 
Safe delivery of the equipment at consignee location 
• 
Physical installation and mounting of the panel 
• 
Electrical and data connectivity 
• 
System configuration and initial setup 
• 
Testing of display, touch, audio, OPS and software 
• 
Ensuring the system is fully operational before handover

o 
Annotation and presentation tools 
o

Basic troubleshooting and system care
