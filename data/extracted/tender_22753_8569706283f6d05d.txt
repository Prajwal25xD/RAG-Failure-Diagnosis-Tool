The bidder must have one office in Gujarat. Please upload the copy of any one of the 
following: Property tax bill/Electricity Bill/Telephone Bill/GST Registration/Lease

submission, they will have to submit undertaking letter to open office in Gujarat within 45 
days after award of the contract. 
• The bidder should be authorized by its OEM for Apple Laptop to quote the bid. (bid no. 
must be mentioned). 
• The bidder must have successfully executed/completed at least  
o Single order of  Rs. 4 Lakhs OR

o 3 orders each of Rs. 1.5 Lakhs
