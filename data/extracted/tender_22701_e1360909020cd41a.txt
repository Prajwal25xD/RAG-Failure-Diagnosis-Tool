Section 2.20.1.1 – Payment Terms, 
Page 11
"The payment will be released quarterly 
on actual usage of Service as mentioned in 
Detailed BOQ of Financial table."
Since the underlying Google Cloud services (Google Vision API / OCR, 
Gemini LLM, and Embedding APIs) are billed by Google to the service 
provider on a monthly basis, a quarterly payment cycle creates a 
structural cash flow mismatch for the bidder. The bidder is required to 
pre-fund Google API costs for up to three months before receiving any 
reimbursement from GIL, which increases working capital burden and 
financial risk — particularly given that consumption volumes are not 
guaranteed. It is therefore requested that the payment cycle be revised 
from quarterly to monthly, based on actual usage reported and verified 
at the end of each calendar month. This will bring the payment terms in 
alignment with the billing cycle of the underlying cloud platform and 
reduce the financial risk premium that bidders would otherwise be 
required to build into their quoted rates.
No Change
