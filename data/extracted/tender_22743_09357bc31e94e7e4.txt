EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (EoI)  FOR EMPANELMENT OF ADVOCATES/LAW FIRMS   
FOR   GUJARAT FIBRE GRID NETWORK LIMITED  
  
The Gujarat Government has setup an SPV namely “Gujarat Fibre Grid 
Network Limited (GFGNL)” to implement Phase-II of BharatNet Project in 
Gujarat. It was established in 2016 and It is registered under the Companies 
Act, 2013 This SPV has been created to synergize with the efforts of 
Government of India under the National Optical Fibre Network and Digital 
India initiative and make focused efforts to actualize a state-to-village fibre 
grid and to facilitate building common Government owned infrastructure to 
provide internet facilities to residents of State of Gujarat. 
           The ‘Amended BharatNet Phase-III Program’ in Gujarat, commanded 
by Gujarat Fibre Grid Network Limited (GFGNL), is a transformative initiative 
aimed at revolutionizing rural connectivity and strengthening digital 
inclusivity. This comprehensive project encompasses the integration, 
expansion, and technological upgrade of BharatNet Phase-I and Phase-II 
networks, laying the foundation for a robust broadband infrastructure. The 
project endeavors to connect approximately 4,400 additional villages over 
and above the existing rural connectivity, empowering rural communities 
through enhanced digital services. Leveraging IP-MPLS technology, ring 
topology, and strategic network design, the upgraded BharatNet Network is 
poised to bridge the digital divide, drive socio-economic development, and 
propel Gujarat into a new era of digital empowerment. 
        Taking into consideration the complex needs in this direction, the Gujrat 
fibre Grid Network Limited (hereinafter referred as GFGNL) is looking for 
empanelment of competent & experienced Advocates & Law firms at its 
registered 
office 
at 
Ahmedabad/ 
Gandhinagar. 
The 
services 
of 
Advocates/Law firms will be mainly utilized in the matters/litigation work 
being contemplated at various forums i.e. Hon’ble Supreme Court of India, 
Hon’ble High Court of Gujarat , District Courts, Industrial Tribunals, National  
Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, State Consumer Commission, 
District Consumer Forum and various Commissions & Other Quasi-judicial 
bodies in matters relating to contract made with vendors and contractual 
employees , Arbitration matter, RTI matter at SIC, etc., within Gujarat as well 
as outside Gujarat region.

GFGNL associates or other agencies etc. besides other litigation

GFGNL as and when referred.

MoAs, MoUs Response on media complaints etc.

advocates/law Firms from the empaneled list or outside the list, in 
its sole discretion.

The Advocate/Law Firm shall not advise any party or accept any

to litigation against the GFGNL.

8.2

the court is a must. His/her absence in the Court, without any 
reasonable ground and notice in advance, will not be accepted.  
8.3 
GFGNL sends the information to the panel advocates through email 
regarding entrustment of a case and after receiving the e-mail, it is 
duty of the panel Advocates to collect the brief/copy of petition 
along with assignment letter from the concerned office of GFGNL at 
the earliest.   
8.4 
GFGNL is free to engage any advocate of its own choice and an 
empaneled Advocate shall make no claim that he/she alone should 
be entrusted with GFGNL's legal matter(s).   
8.5 
Refusal by any advocate/Law Firm to accept any work without any 
reasonable cause (e.g. on grounds of conflict of interest) may entail 
removal of such advocate from the panel, forthwith without waiting 
for the empanelment period to expire.  
8.6 
The advocates/Law Firms shall accept the terms and conditions of 
the empanelment as determined by the GFGNL from time to time.   
8.7 
In order to ensure that there is effective check on the cases being 
conducted, the Advocates/Law Firms on the panel must report the 
status of the cases after each date of hearing. Failure to submit 
status report will be a ground for removal of the name from the 
panel.   
8.8 
In cases where on the request of the Department of Science and 
Technology, GOG have also to be represented, no extra fee shall be 
paid to the advocate. i. If required render all assistance to special or 
senior Counsel engaged in a particular case before the High Court 
and other judicial bodies.  
8.9 
To Keep GFGNL informed and updated on all-important

court on the date of its pronouncement, supplying copy of orders 
/judgment etc.  
8.10 Furnish statement about the cases represented by him/her before 
the concerned courts or any other authority and their outcomes.  
8.11 When any case attended by him/her is decided against the 
Organization, the Advocate/Law firm  concerned must give 
considered opinion regarding the reasons for such adverse order

than 5 working days of the order (kuchha copy).  30% of the fees

9.2.1 Giving false information in the application for empanelment;   
9.2.2 Failing to attend the hearing of the case without any sufficient
