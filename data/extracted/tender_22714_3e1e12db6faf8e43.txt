Bidder Scope (for Office Suite) 
The bidder shall supply, install, activate, configure, and commission the Office licenses at the designated 
Government office(s). The bidder shall provide technical support and facilitate upgrades, updates, and security 
patches as applicable under the license, for a period of five (5) years from the date of installation.

1.2 Bidder Past Performance criteria 
The Bidder should have successfully supplied a cumulative quantity of at least 2,500 license units of similar items 
(Supply of Office Productivity Software Licenses such as MS Office, Polaris Office, etc.) to any Central / State 
Government Organization / PSU/ Public Listed Company/ Higher Educational Institutions recognized by UGC or 
Act of Central or State Govt. within the last 7 years before the bid opening date. Cumulative quantities of all such 
work orders during the last 7 years will be considered for evaluation. 
 
Documentary evidence: Copy of purchase Orders  
1.3 Manufacturer's Authorization Form (MAF) 
The Bidder must submit a valid Manufacturer's Authorization Form (MAF) / OEM Authorization Certificate from 
the respective OEM for the software licenses being offered. The authorization shall confirm that the offered 
licenses are genuine and include OEM support and entitlement to the latest upgrades, updates, and security 
patches for a period of five (5) years from the date of installation.

1 The Bidder may submit a bid for either the Polaris Office Suite, or the Microsoft Office Suite, or both. However, 
Bidder shall submit a valid Manufacturer's Authorization Form (MAF) / OEM Authorization Certificate issued 
by the respective OEM, in accordance with the provisions of this bid document. 
2 The Contract for Hardware Items (Laptop, Desktop Computer and All-in-One Desktop) and Software Items 
(Polaris Office Suite/ Microsoft Office Suite) shall be evaluated independently. The award of Contract for 
Hardware Items and Software Items shall also be carried out separately. 


3 The quantity of licenses indicated in this Corrigendum is purely indicative/notional. The actual procurement 
and supply of licenses shall be made as per the requirements, need and ask from the respective Government 
Departments/Organizations during the validity of the Contract. 
4 Bidders participating in the Hardware Items category may also participate in the Software Items category, 
subject to fulfilling the prescribed eligibility criteria and all other requirements applicable to the respective 
Office Suite category. However, the technical evaluation, financial evaluation, and award of Contract for the 
Software Items shall be carried out independently of the Hardware Items. 
5 Evaluation Method: Evaluation shall be carried out separately for each item category. The bidder quoting the 
lowest price (L1) for the respective item category shall be selected. 
 
 
(Please note: A non-refundable Bid Processing Fee of ₹17,700/- shall be applicable per bid, irrespective of the 
number of item categories quoted. The Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) (refundable) shall be applicable 
separately for each item category quoted. Submission of both the Bid Processing Fee and EMD is mandatory.)
