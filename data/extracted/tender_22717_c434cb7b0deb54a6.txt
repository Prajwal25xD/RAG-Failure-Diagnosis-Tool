Scope of Work:  
1. The bidder shall supply, install, configure, test and commission the proposed Hyper-Converged 
Infrastructure (HCI), Network, Security, Software and all associated hardware/software 
components, including required licenses, for the GUDM e-Nagar Project. 
2. The proposed infrastructure shall be deployed for UAT and Development environments and shall 
support application testing, validation and performance assessment activities. 
3. The bidder shall provide minimum 3-nodes for proposed HCI solution with required compute, 
storage, networking and security components, including all active and passive accessories, as per 
the technical specifications of the tender. 
4. The bidder shall provision and configure the proposed infrastructure to support the envisaged 
workload of the GUDM e-Nagar application ecosystem and future scalability requirements. 
5. The proposed solution shall support migration, deployment and integration of applications from 
the UAT and Development environment to the GSDC Production Environment. The bidder shall 
provide all necessary assistance, tools, accessories, configuration support and technical services 
required for seamless migration of workloads, applications, virtual machines, containers and 
associated data. 
6. The proposed solution shall support migration and deployment of applications from the 
UAT/Development environment to the GSDC Production Environment and shall be 
compatible with the VMware Tanzu platform deployed at GSDC. The bidder shall provide 
all necessary migration, integration, testing and deployment support without any 
additional cost.  
7. Any Hardware, software, accessories, tools configuration, interoperability or migration-related 
activities required for successful deployment in the VMware Tanzu environment shall be in the 
scope of the bidder without any additional cost to GUDM. 
8. The bidder shall provide and configure a dedicated 1 GPU based single node with capacity of 48 
GB or higher for AI/ML, analytics and future application requirements. 
9. The bidder shall provide and configure the UTM/Next Generation Firewall, network switches, 
smart rack, UPS, cooling, monitoring, and all other supporting infrastructure required for 
successful operation of the solution. Any electrification required will be extended by the bidder. 
10. The bidder shall provide necessary support during application testing, user acceptance testing, 
performance validation and issue resolution related to the supplied infrastructure. 
11. The bidder shall ensure integration of the proposed infrastructure with the existing GSDC/GUDM 
network and security environment and comply with all applicable policies, standards and 
guidelines. Any security, vulnerabilities identified by GSOC/GSDC in hardware or application has 
to be resolved in co-ordination with application and security team. 
12. The bidder shall submit OEM certifications, technical documentation, architecture diagrams, 
SOPs, troubleshooting guides, knowledge transfer documents and other relevant documents for 
the deployed solution. 
13. The bidder shall conduct Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), installation, commissioning and 
operational acceptance of the proposed solution in coordination with GUDM/GIL and obtain 
necessary sign-offs. 
14. The bidder shall rectify all discrepancies observed during FAT, testing, commissioning or 
acceptance activities and ensure successful completion of the project as per the contractual 
requirements. 
15. The bidder shall provide comprehensive OEM warranty, support services and maintenance for a 
period of five (5) years from the date of successful FAT, including software updates, hardware 
replacement, technical support and performance assurance of the complete solution.
