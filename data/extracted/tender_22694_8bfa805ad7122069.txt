Clarifications 
1.Section 10, Clause XIV: Rack at Sub-Block/Shelter Locations (Phase-I & Phase-II) 
The clause requiring “sufficient perforation for ventilation” in racks has been identified as 
a typographical error and therefore stands deleted. The other applicable rack 
specifications shall be remaining as per RFP. 
2.Annexure-A, TEC-GR Standards List, Line-Item 20 
The reference to TEC 66140:2019 was inadvertently retained in the Master RFP in this 
section, However, it was deleted in technical specification table. Hence, This standard 
stands deleted. 
3.Clarification on Rack Certification Requirements 
To promote broader competition and participation, bidders may submit 
TEC/TSEC/QF103 certification for either a 24U rack or a 48U rack. Certification of 
any one of the specified rack sizes shall be considered acceptable for bid evaluation for 
both 24U and 48U rack. Bidder shall require to certify QA/TSEC for additional test cases 
for the offered rack during the execution from the concerned authority. The rack 
provisions as per technical specification in the RFP, i.e. without sensor/smart feature 
shall be complied. 
4.Clarification on UPS Configuration 
Both “UPS systems with built-in galvanic isolation” and “UPS systems using external 
galvanic isolation transformers” shall be accepted, provided that the proposed solution 
maintains a minimum of 20% free rack space in the deployed rack on field.
