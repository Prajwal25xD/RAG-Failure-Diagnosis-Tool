We are building RAG Detective, an AI-powered tool that collects tender data from websites and PDFs, extracts and stores the text and embeddings in PostgreSQL, and automatically diagnoses why a RAG system gives incorrect 
answers—whether due to OCR, chunking, retrieval, conflicting information, or reasoning errors.
